# Launcher 0.6.1.0

This release gives the launcher a background with depth that moves with your mouse, sends the space station off on its nuclear engine when you launch the game, lets you stop the game from the launcher, and fixes the news and update dialogs cutting off what they show.

## New Content

### Background

* Added: A layered background where the stars, the planet and the space station shift slightly as the mouse moves, giving the scene depth. The glass panels move with it
* Added: A Background motion setting in the Settings tab to turn the movement off
* Added: When the game starts, the space station fires up its nuclear engine and flies off screen, and comes back once the game has closed
* Updated: The stars are now drawn by the launcher, so they stay sharp at any window size

### Launching

* Added: While the game is running, the main button turns into a cancel button that stops the game. It asks first, since anything unsaved is lost
* Fixed: Launching through Steam left the button saying LAUNCH while the game was starting. It now shows the cancel button until the game closes, the same as launching directly
* Fixed: Launching directly left the button dark green and unclickable until the game closed

### Command line tool

* Added: `settings set --background-motion on` or `off`, and `settings` shows it
* Added: `snapshot` is accepted anywhere a release channel is asked for

## Bug Fixes & QoL Improvements

* Fixed: Images in news posts were squished sideways in the narrow launcher. They now scale down and keep their shape
* Fixed: The update dialog cut the changelog off at 500 characters. It now shows all of it and scrolls, at the same size as before
* Updated: The beta channel is now called Snapshot everywhere in the launcher and the command line tool
* Updated: The macOS download is now named `Ksp2Redux-macos-arm64.dmg`, matching the other downloads

## Tools & Infrastructure

* Updated: The launcher's images are now WebP, making the download about 3 MB smaller
